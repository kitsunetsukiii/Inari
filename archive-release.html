archive 2022-2023 release for sept 19th 2026 market

the next closet shop - sept 19th - i am finally releasing the last of my 'archive' work from 2022 - 2023 ive sold a few pieces here and there over the past few years but have had it packed away for a little while. this next closet shop will be my biggest, in terms of volume. so that i can finally be done with that era. all the bits and pieces of broken torn and tattered survivors of its history. hundred year old pieces of fabric and lace.

i have a hundred year old maid dress from 1910-1930 with the apron. im borrowing the auction photos from when i bought it in 2021. it was my first antique auction purchase. they lost lace extras that it was supposed to come with. i have got the important parts atleast. i found an etsy listing of the same uniform once, but i cant find it anymore. i remember reading that it was used for a particular hotel chain at that time.

the bleeding immaculate heart series, made from upcycled vintage textiles.

featuring handsewn one of a kind "bleeding" and "immaculate" hearts. flowers or angel wings and pierced scar of longinus

alongside other upcycled vintage, and reworked knitwear skirts
i wont be making pieces like this again. i cant do the upcycle rework stuff anymore. its a skill of its own. i appreciate those who can, its just not for me. im much better with a machine + raw materials. if i ever release these again in the future i wont be the one constructing them. all the tedious hand work bits and beads, my mind is not structured enough for it. i need the rigidity of a machine to control the creative chaos. <3 i love my machines <3
